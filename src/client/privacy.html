HavenWorld Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 | Version 1.0 (Closed Alpha)

HavenWorld ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy while providing a rich, social virtual world experience. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights under GDPR and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we store your chosen username and a securely salted bcrypt hash of your password. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Game Activity & World State: In-game avatar coordinates, room decorations, inventory items, virtual currency transactions, and chat messages are processed to maintain real-time multiplayer synchronization.
  • Technical & Network Logs: IP addresses, browser client user-agent strings, and request timestamps are logged temporarily for rate limiting, DDoS protection, and safety moderation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain persistent lofts and avatars across sessions.
  • Relay real-time position updates, emotes, and chat between players via WebSockets.
  • Enforce community guidelines, prevent harassment, and block automated spambots.
  • Analyze server performance, tick rates, and crash reports.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure Services

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to securely host HavenWorld:

  • Cloudflare: Provides network-level DDoS shielding, CDN asset delivery, and encrypted tunnel routing.
  • Supabase / PostgreSQL: Provides encrypted relational database storage when cloud persistence is configured.
  • Sentry / Uptime Monitoring: Collects anonymous application error stack traces to fix bugs promptly.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize player data to third-party advertising networks.

4. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your location, HavenWorld respects your personal data rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of your stored player profile and inventory data.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated room/furniture data at any time.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy-related inquiries, data export, or account deletion requests, please contact our data team at privacy@havenworld.me.
